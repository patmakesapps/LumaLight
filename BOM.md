# LumaLight bill of materials

Everything to buy for one LumaLight. Wiring is in `README.md`; positions, print orientation and
assembly are in `cad/LUMALIGHT_SPEC.md`.

## Electronics

| Qty | Part | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ESP32 dev board (`esp32dev`), USB-C | Hangs on the back panel, USB end down |
| 1 | CQRobot VL53L1X time-of-flight sensor, 23 x 28.5 mm board | With its 6-pin JST cable |
| 1 | 12-LED NeoPixel ring, GRB | Pocket fits a 36.8 mm ring (Adafruit 1643 size) |
| ~7 | Female-to-female jumper leads | Sensor and ring to the ESP32 pins |
| 1 | USB-C cable and USB power adapter | Leaves through the notch in the back panel |

## Hardware

| Qty | Part | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | M2.5 x 6 self-tapping screw | ESP32 top pair, sensor board |
| 6 | M2.5 x 8 self-tapping screw | ESP32 USB-end pair, back panel to body |

No inserts and no magnets. Every screw is driven along its axis from outside the part.
